Inside "Lisenskontoret", the licence bureau of a small Norwegian seaside town, seen from the side like an adventure-game room. Four old wooden counters in a row, each with a glass window and a large painted number 1, 2, 3 and 4 above it. A queue-ticket machine on a stand, a ticking wall clock, towering stacks of forms, a potted rubber plant, rubber stamps on a carousel and a big red ink pad. Behind counter 1 stands the clerk: a stout man in his sixties with a grey walrus moustache, half-moon glasses, a cardigan and sleeve garters, holding a huge rubber stamp in the air with a look that is stern but secretly amused. His head and shoulders are clearly visible. The only readable text is the numbers 1 to 4.
