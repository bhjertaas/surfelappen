A single building on its own, for pasting into the town map: a small, dignified old Norwegian savings bank, seen from above at a 3/4 isometric angle exactly like a city-builder game, with its front facade facing down and to the left and its right side wall visible, matching a town map viewed from the upper right. Pale cream stone walls with tall arched windows and white trim, a dark slate roof with two small dormer windows, a little green copper cupola with a clock on the roof ridge, a heavy dark wooden double door at the top of a few stone steps at the front, brass lamps beside the door, a cash machine set into the wall. A small strip of cobblestone pavement at its feet. No readable text and no logos. The whole building visible, nothing cut off. Outside the building and pavement, a perfectly flat solid pure magenta background (#FF00FF), nothing else.
