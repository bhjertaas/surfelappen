A comic illustration for a lesson about influencers and the "perfect life": a single wide scene split down the middle. On the left, glossy and glowing: a young woman posing for a selfie in front of a rented angel-wing wall, a ring light, perfect make-up, a fake ocean-view backdrop. On the right, the reality just outside the frame: the same woman in sweatpants, a messy apartment, a tripod held up by a stack of books, a sponsored product box and a cold cup of coffee. In the foreground, a teenager on a sofa looks at their phone with a slightly glum face. Little floating icons of hearts and follower counts drift up from the phone, without any numbers or words.
