Inside "Gone Phishing", a cramped, cosy bait-and-tackle shop on a pier, seen from the side like an adventure-game room. Fishing rods, nets, buoys and a stuffed fish on the wall; a crooked shelf of lures that look suspiciously like envelopes, padlocks and fake bank cards dangling on hooks; a little old computer on the counter with a glowing screen; a porthole window with the harbour outside. Behind the counter stands the owner: a weathered, good-humoured woman in her sixties with a yellow raincoat, a knitted hat and reading glasses, holding a magnifying glass. The only readable text: a small sign on the counter that reads exactly "GONE PHISHING".
